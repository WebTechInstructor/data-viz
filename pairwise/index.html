How to read it

  • Each point represents one month.
  • Upward patterns suggest positive correlation.
  • Downward patterns suggest negative correlation.
  • Diffuse clouds suggest little linear correlation.
The small r value in each panel summarizes the full 10-year Pearson correlation for that pair.

Why split the decade?

The two marker styles make it easier to see whether an overall relationship remains similar or changes over time. This is a diagnostic view, not a claim that August 2021 is a causal breakpoint.

Series key

FRED codes are retained in the series key for verification. Correlation describes association, not causation.
