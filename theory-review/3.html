Lecture 9 · Suppression
Or hide what matters less
DOI(x) = importance(x) − distance(x, focus)
Generalized fisheye: filter out low-interest items
Z-thru: add or remove whole layers
Magic lens: a movable window that filters
Costs: distortion can be hard to read, and interest must be recalculated every time the focus moves.
After Furnas (1986) · Also: halo (hints at off-screen items) · cone trees + DOI
