E. Trachycaulus Periodicity Explorer

Explore whether weekly incidence in 50 U.S. cities follows repeated rise/fall patterns of 6, 8, 16, or 20 weeks. The overview ranks evidence; the detail views let you inspect the actual weekly behavior.

How the match score works. For each candidate period, half of the cycle is expected to rise and half to fall. Every possible starting alignment is tested, and the score is the percentage of week-to-week changes that agree with the best alignment. Higher scores suggest a stronger fit, but do not prove that a cycle exists.
